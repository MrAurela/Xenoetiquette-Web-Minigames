The known aliens are divided into 6 species based on their appearance.

Dinglebop

A sophisticated hive mind species

Not used to direct confrontation or disagreements their language lacks concept of questions and commands.

Picaglorbors

A simple-minded ooze species

Increase chances of being understood by repeating a variation of your message once per each eye.

Pobgetinks

An Archantian metamorphic species

Always address the female of a the family but never in flirtatious or dominating tone.

Poppabingks

An ancient marine species

Avoid unvoiced consonant such as "P", "T", "K", "F", "S", and "WH" in underwater communication.

Q

a newly discovered species

Any engagement with the species is to be avoided until more information has been collected.

Ticatings

a shapeshifter species

Mischevious but responses with unexpected interest to Earth humour.

OF 02

There is 4 influential political factions operating in the Galaxy. The factions can be most accurately identified by the hat an alien is wearing.

Amazing Future

A technocratic megacorporation

Believes that science can solve more problems than it creates.

Friendship of Everyone

An altruistic alliance

Advocates cooperation between all species and factions, except with Red Dwarves.

Galactic Heroes

An idealistic collective

Supports individuality, freedom, and self-fulfilment.

Red Dwarves

A nihilistic faction

Conservatively follows traditions as there is no free will in a deterministic cosmos.
